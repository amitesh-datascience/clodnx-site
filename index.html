What is ERPNext?

ERPNext is an open-source ERP system that brings accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, HR and projects into one database, so every team works from the same numbers.

There are no per-user licence fees, you can host it yourself or in the cloud, and it can be customised without breaking upgrades. The software is the easy part. Fitting it to your workflows, cleaning your data and training your people is where most projects succeed or fail, and that is the work we do.
